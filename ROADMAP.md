# Roadmap

Shipped: pure-data split/range/batch planning, pause-setting validation, timed-word mapping and framing geometry.

Next: expand property-based and edge-case coverage; document stable snapshot and plan schemas; make source identifiers platform-neutral; build a separately reviewed editor adapter with recoverable execution.

Later: optional provider-neutral AI planning and transcript workflows, with explicit cost controls and truthful capability reporting. Neither provider integration nor a full editor is included today.

Public milestones will reflect delivered code, not private development history. No completion date is promised.
